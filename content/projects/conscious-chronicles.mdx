## What it is

A publication of long-form essays on consciousness, philosophy and science —
*The Mirror That Cannot See Itself*, *The River With No Banks*, *The Fear of
Being Understood*. Pieces more interested in asking a question well than in
answering it quickly.

The project here is the writing. Every essay is mine: researched, drafted,
edited, published. It's the clearest sample of the editorial work I take on for
other people, and unlike a case study it's been sitting in public for anyone to
judge.

## Why it isn't built from scratch

It runs on Wix. I could have built it in Next.js — Saroj Vidyalaya and Kisan
Sathi are both mine, so the capability isn't the constraint. I'd make the same
call again.

A personal essay publication needs a domain, decent typography, a blog engine
and a way to publish on a Tuesday evening without a deploy. That's a solved
problem. Building it myself would have bought me control I don't need over
details that don't affect a single reader, at the cost of the only resource
that actually limits this project, which is writing time.

I keep saying on this site that I'll tell you when you don't need the thing
you're asking me to build. It would be strange to say that and then quietly hold
myself to a different standard because a custom build looks better in a
portfolio. So: the right tool for this one was a website builder, and the
interesting work was elsewhere.

## Why it's here

Two reasons, and neither is engineering.

It's the evidence for the editorial half of what I do — several years of
published, self-edited long-form writing on genuinely difficult material.

And it's a worked example of the judgement I'm actually selling. Deciding *not*
to build something is a real deliverable. Most of the value I add on a build
engagement arrives in the first week, when I tell someone which parts of their
idea are worth the money.
